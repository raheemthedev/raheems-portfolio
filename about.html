/ ABOUT A

about me

Request résumé ↗

I’m an independent designer and developer based in Toronto, creating brand identities, campaigns, motion, and digital experiences for ambitious people and growing businesses.

My practice moves between strategy, visual design, and development— building clear systems with enough character to stay memorable.

I work closely with founders, studios, and creative teams from early direction through final execution.

/ PRACTICE

I specialize in identity systems, art direction, motion, and creative development—building expressive ideas into useful digital experiences.

I’m equally interested in the quiet systems behind the work: research, structure, rhythm, and the details that make an experience feel inevitable.

/ PERSPECTIVE

I see design as a meeting point between expression, intention, and utility. I move between strategy, identity, motion, and development to build systems that communicate clearly and stay memorable.
